## 1. Privacy Policy / Notice

**Effective: [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]] · Controller: [[LEGAL_ENTITY]] ("Angaza360", "we") · DPO: [[DPO_NAME]], [[DPO_EMAIL]] · ODPC registration: [[ODPC_REG_NO]]**

**1.1 Who we are and our role.** Angaza360 is a business-management platform for Kenyan MSMEs. For your *account and the business you register*, we are the **data controller**. For the data your business enters about *its own* customers, employees and suppliers, your business is the controller and **we are your data processor**, processing it under your instruction and our Terms.

**1.2 What we collect.**
- *Account & verification:* owner's full legal name, National ID/passport number and a scanned copy (front and back), personal and business KRA PIN, phone, email, business name, registration details, sector, physical address and county.
- *Business data you enter:* products, sales, inventory, customers, suppliers, payroll and employee records, accounting entries, tax data.
- *Payment-integration data:* M-Pesa transaction references and status from your own M-Pesa shortcode. **We do not collect or store card numbers or M-Pesa PINs.**
- *Technical:* IP address, device/browser information, and security and audit logs.

**1.3 Why we collect it and our lawful basis (s.29, s.30).**
- To **provide the service** (your contract with us) — account, POS, inventory, accounting, payroll, reporting.
- To **verify your identity and business** (legal obligation + our legitimate interest in preventing fraud) — the ID number and document.
- To **meet legal/tax obligations** (legal obligation) — KRA eTIMS and statutory records.
- To **secure the platform** (legitimate interest) — audit logs, MFA, fraud prevention.
- For **optional communications** (consent) — product updates; you may opt out at any time.

**1.4 Who we share it with (recipients & safeguards).** Only as needed to run the service, under contract and appropriate safeguards: **Safaricom** (M-Pesa payments; SMS where applicable), **Africa's Talking** (SMS), our **email provider** (transactional email via `[[LEGAL_ENTITY]]`-controlled mailboxes), the **Kenya Revenue Authority** (eTIMS, at your request), **Google Cloud** (hosting), and **Cloudflare** (DNS/CDN/security). We do not sell your personal data. A mentor or donor programme sees your figures **only with your explicit, logged consent**, which you can withdraw at any time.

**1.5 Cross-border transfers (s.48–49).** Our hosting may process data on infrastructure located outside Kenya. Where this occurs we apply appropriate safeguards and transfer only on a lawful basis. `[[Confirm the Google Cloud region and the transfer basis with legal review.]]`

**1.6 How long we keep it (s.39).** We keep account and business data while your account is active and for a defined period afterwards. We retain **financial, tax and statutory records for the period Kenyan law requires** (tax records are generally kept for at least five years). Identity-verification documents are kept only as long as needed for verification and legal defence, then securely deleted, per our retention schedule.

**1.7 How we protect it (s.41).** Encryption of credentials and identity documents; access controls and role-based permissions; multi-factor authentication for administrators; audit logging; private, non-public storage of uploaded documents; and least-privilege support access that is reason-logged, time-limited and notified to you.

**1.8 Your rights (s.26, s.34–40).** You may be **informed**, **access** your data (within 7 days), have it **rectified** (14 days), **erased** (14 days, where applicable), **restrict** or **object** to processing, and request **portability** (30 days) — the platform's data-export feature supports access and portability. For direct marketing you may **object or opt out at any time** (s.37). To exercise a right, contact [[DPO_EMAIL]].

**1.9 Consequences of not providing data.** Some data is required to open and verify an account or to meet legal obligations; without it we may be unable to provide the service or a specific feature.

**1.10 Complaints.** Contact our DPO at [[DPO_EMAIL]] first. You may also lodge a complaint with the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner** (odpc.go.ke).

**1.11 Changes.** We will post changes here with a new effective date and notify you of material changes.

## 3. Cookie Policy

**Effective: [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]**

We use cookies and similar technologies only as needed to run the service and, with your consent, to understand usage.
- **Strictly necessary** (always on): sign-in/session, security (CSRF), and your working preferences. These cannot be switched off without breaking the service.

You can manage non-essential cookies via the banner or your browser settings. Cookie types we use include session and persistent, first-party (and, for analytics only, the provider's). For more on how we handle personal data, see the Privacy Policy.

## 5. Internal / organisational obligations (YOUR actions — not web pages)

These are compliance tasks for you and your legal reviewer, surfaced per the DPA; they are **not** built by the platform:
- **ODPC registration** as a data controller **and** processor (2021 Regulations; certificate valid 24 months) — likely required for a fintech/SaaS at scale. Put `[[ODPC_REG_NO]]` in the Privacy Policy once issued.
- **Designate a DPO** (s.24) and publish their contact.
- **DPIA** (s.31) before high-risk processing (identity documents, financial profiling) — complete before go-live.
- **Breach-response procedure + register** (s.43) — notify the ODPC within **72 hours** of a harmful breach.
- **Record of Processing Activities (RoPA)** and a written **retention/disposal schedule**, reviewed every 12 months.
- **Data-processing terms with subprocessors** (Safaricom, Africa's Talking, email host, Google Cloud, Cloudflare).
